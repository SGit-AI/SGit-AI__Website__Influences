The source documents

This site will be written from a commissioning pack, and the pack will be published in full, because a site that argues from a document should let you read the document. It has not landed in the tree yet.

The raw markdown is the source of truth. Each reader page renders its document from the raw file in briefs/ at load time; if that fails, the page falls back to a link to the raw file. Nothing is transcribed by hand, so a reader page cannot say something the document does not.

Every file is fetchable at a stable constructed URL: /briefs/<filename>. That is a promise, not an accident.

No source documents are published yet. This release is the pipeline, not the register: validation, auto-tagging and deployment are live and proven, and the commissioning pack this site will be written from has not been unpacked into the tree. When it is, it arrives here in full — raw markdown in briefs/, one reader page each, generated from data/documents.json.

That is the order on purpose. A site whose release gate works can publish anything safely; a site full of pages with no gate cannot.

Licence, and the split that matters here

Every page of this site is released under CC BY 4.0. Attribution: Dinis Cruz, with AI co-authorship (Claude, Anthropic). The anchor works are not: every work this site will say shaped the thinking belongs to its author, and is linked rather than rehosted. The release gate enforces that rather than the site merely stating it — which is why the gate shipped before any of the entries did.

influences.sgit.ai

A site in the sgit.ai network: the people, works, topics and things that shaped the founder's thinking, and the places in the estate where that shaping can be checked. An influence entry here is a falsifiable claim about the codebase, not a reading list. The site's own words are CC BY 4.0; the anchor works belong to their authors and are linked, never rehosted.
