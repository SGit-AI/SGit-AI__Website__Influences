Comms: tasks, requests and the briefing queue

The working channel between the project lead and the agent building this site, kept in public and updated every release. On the sibling sites this page carries build questions. Here it does one more job: the founder's stated plan — in time I will create a detailed briefing document about each one — becomes a visible, addressable inbox, with one open thread per influence awaiting a briefing. When a document lands, the thread closes and the entry's tier moves.

The briefing queue — one thread per influence awaiting a document

Seven influences are on the founder's own list and have no corpus evidence at all. That is not a defect in the mining: they are the things nobody else can research. Each gets a thread here, and the entry stays a published stub with its research plan visible until the thread closes.

Suggested order, and why. Start with N1 or N6 — Neil Peart and the band years are the entries no researcher can substitute for, and the two most likely to change how the rest of the register reads. The others have public material a research pass can work from once the founder confirms the anchor.

#Influence, and what the briefing needs to settleState
N1Neil Peart & Rush. The anchor: which record, which lyric, which period. The hypothesis to test rather than assert — the craftsman-lyricist archetype, technical mastery in service of meaning. Note the boundary: this entry describes and links. Lyrics are the one place where even a verse is a legal risk, and the entry quotes at most a fragment under analysis.for the founder
N2Kevin Kelly's books. Which is the anchor — What Technology Wants, The Inevitable, or the 1,000 True Fans essay? And is the technium-to-Wardley-evolution connection real or a plausible-looking coincidence?for the founder
N3Simon Sinek — the golden circle. Anchor: the book or the talk? The site network is organised why-first, which is either evidence or hindsight, and only the founder can say which.for the founder
N4The Cathedral and the Bazaar. The essay's factual history belongs to open-source.sgit.ai. This entry owns only what it did to the founder, and that is exactly the part not written down anywhere.for the founder
N5David Rice — "Upon the Threshold of Opportunity". Which year's OWASP keynote is canonical, and is there a recording whose licence permits an embed? OWASP's own channels are the preferred source; a commercial conference recording gets a link, not an embed.for the founder
N6Music, and playing in a band. Entirely unresearchable by anyone else. The hypothesis worth testing: band-playing as the original experience of real-time collaborative flow — the thing the estate's development methodology tries to recreate in software.for the founder
N7Diverse and distributed teams. Research plan is written: the founder's management writing, the estate's team-roles system, and OWASP's global-chapter model as the formative experience. The briefing needs to say which of those is the origin and which is the consequence.for the founder

Questions — these need an answer from a person

#QuestionState
Q1The full text of the Bret Victor register. The commissioning pack ships a faithful summary of its structure and closing argument; the full text lives with the founder, supplied in conversation and not retained across a context boundary. Entry one cannot ship complete without it — the site publishes what the pack carries, clearly marked as the summary, and swaps in the original the moment it arrives. This is the first ask.for the founder
Q2Confirm or demote the discovered influences. Luhmann, Christopher Alexander, Team Topologies/Cynefin and Vannevar Bush were found by mining the corpus and never named by the founder. Do they belong, or are any of them a citation rather than an influence? The falsifiability of this site starts with the founder falsifying its discoveries. Design and Steve Jobs: already confirmed, 25 August 2026 — the first discovered → confirmed transition, before the site existed.for the founder
Q2bThe Design entry's anchor. Which artefact is canonical — the "design is how it works" formulation, the MP3-to-CD whiteboard story, a keynote, Ive's process, a specific product? And when did Design-with-a-capital-D become explicit in the thinking rather than implicit in the work?for the founder
Q3Anchors to confirm across the stated seven — Kelly, Sinek, Rice. Rolled into the briefing threads above rather than asked twice.merged into N2/N3/N5
Q4Are there influences you rejected? Things everyone assumes shaped the work but did not, or that were outgrown. An entry with status: counter-influence would be unusual on the web and very much in this site's spirit — a register that can only add is not falsifiable in the direction that matters.for the founder
Q5Where did graphs actually enter? The founding obsession has an entire sibling site and no recorded origin. O2 Platform's code-flow work, or earlier? The link-out entry owns the personal history and nothing else, so this is the only thing it needs.for the founder
Q6Does the map belong here or on the graphs sibling once it becomes a real G³ demonstration? Proposal: authored here, mirrored there — the influence graph is this site's content, and its rendering is that site's subject.for the lead

The rule this site adopts: likenesses are original artwork only, never scraped photographs and never AI renderings presented as portraits; idea-figures are redrawn in house style with the idea's author attributed. That needs either an artist pass or a generation workflow the founder approves. Tracked as T13.for the founder

Requests — build-side, for the lead

#RequestState
R1The corpus is cited, not resolved. Every entry lists the files that put it in its tier, by path. This repository holds the website, not the corpus, so those paths are quoted from the mining run behind the commissioning pack (v0.33.62, 25 August 2026) rather than checked against the repositories at build time. Nothing on the site can disagree with the pack; the site can and will drift from the estate. Closing it needs a decision: give this build read access to the corpus and re-run the scan as a build step, or accept a dated snapshot and re-scan per release.for the lead
R2Trace rows without versions. The register format's own rule is that a trace table without version numbers is an opinion. The mining run reached files and named them; it did not always record the release they were read at. Rows are marked where a version is missing and the entry says how many — but the real fix is the same access R1 asks for.for the lead
R3Two decades of earlier material are unmined. The corpus scanned for this pack is the current estate plus the current docs site. The O2 Platform-era blog, OWASP talks, conference decks and the earlier books were not reachable — and they are exactly where the stated influences' traces will be found. The per-entry research plans say where to look; someone has to be able to look.for the lead
R4GitHub Pages environment. The workflow calls configure-pages with enablement: true and deploys from dev, which is this repository's default branch. On the sibling sites the first deploy job failed until the github-pages environment recognised the branch. This release is the test.testing

Notes from the build

  • The pipeline was built first, deliberately. Every sibling site's early releases are the same story: content lands, then something in the release machinery turns out to be wrong, and the fix is a release of its own. Publishing an almost-empty site through the full gate proves the gate on a tree small enough to read in one sitting.
  • Two of the six checks are editorial, and that is the point. The no-verbatim gate and the register-is-the-data check encode rules this site would otherwise merely state in a footer. A site whose thesis is that a claim should be checkable is in a poor position to publish unchecked claims about itself.
  • One trap was inherited and pre-empted. The Python .gitignore this repository starts from carries build/, which silently swallows admin/build/. A sibling site shipped a first release whose validate job died on a missing file before it could check anything — the checks appeared to run and did not. The !admin/build/ negation is in this repository's first commit.
  • Nothing here claims to be finished. The nav lists the pages that exist, the version badge says what stage this is, and the release history says what shipped in one row per release. All three are easier to grow than to walk back.

influences.sgit.ai

A site in the sgit.ai network: the people, works, topics and things that shaped the founder's thinking, and the places in the estate where that shaping can be checked. An influence entry here is a falsifiable claim about the codebase, not a reading list. The site's own words are CC BY 4.0; the anchor works belong to their authors and are linked, never rehosted.
