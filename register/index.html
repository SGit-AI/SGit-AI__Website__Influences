The register

Every influence, in three tiers by how well the claim is evidenced. 25 entries: 15 traced, 7 stated, 3 discovered and still awaiting Dinis Cruz's confirmation. The counts are computed from data/influences.json on every build, not typed — because tier movement is the one event this site exists to record.

An entry is a falsifiable claim, not an appreciation. Anyone can say Bret Victor was an influence. The register says which of Victor's patterns appear where in the estate, at which version, and which are still missing — specified precisely enough to build. The format, in full →

influences.sgit.ai

The influence register of Dinis Cruz — the people, works, topics and things that shaped his thinking, and the places in his own code where that shaping can be checked. An influence entry here is a falsifiable claim about a codebase, not a reading list. A site in the sgit.ai network. The site's own words are CC BY 4.0; the anchor works belong to their authors and are linked, never rehosted.
