David Rice — “Upon the Threshold of Opportunity”

stated presentation stub

An OWASP keynote, named by the founder from memory. The one entry whose anchor is a specific recording that has to be found before it can be linked.

Block 1 The anchor

Upon the Threshold of Opportunity — an OWASP AppSec keynote — David Rice

No canonical URL. Not every anchor has one address — a book, a practice or a personal era does not — and this site links what is linkable rather than inventing a canonical home for something that has none. The wider library below carries what can be linked.

Year and recording unconfirmed. Locating the canonical recording is part of the research plan rather than a detail — N5.

Block 2 The founder's words

No first-person statement about this influence exists in the corpus yet. What follows is the site's reading of the evidence, not the founder's words — and it stays labelled as such until his briefing document arrives.

A conference talk is an unusual anchor and a revealing one. Books get re-read; a keynote is heard once, and an influence that survives that is an influence that landed at the right moment. What the entry needs is not a summary of Rice's argument — that is available — but why this talk, to this person, in that room, which is the part only the founder holds.

Nests under OWASP, where the community-as-influence claim lives.

Block 3 The principle

(hypothesis) Security is an economic and societal opportunity rather than a fear industry — the argument of Geekonomics, made to a room of practitioners.

Constructed from Rice's published book rather than from the keynote, because the keynote has not been located. It is a placeholder for the real principle, not the real principle.
